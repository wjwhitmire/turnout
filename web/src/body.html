Method and limits
  • Money is whole cents. Line amount = quantity x rate, rounded half away from zero; a percent discount is shared across lines by the largest remainder method; tax is rounded once per document or once per line, as you choose. Optional quote lines count only when the client chooses them.
  • Schedules use calendar dates and wall-clock times with no time zone, so an 8:00 AM weekly visit stays at 8:00 across daylight saving changes. Monthly visits on a day past the month's end fall on its last day (the 31st is February 28 or 29). Weeks run Monday to Sunday; every N weeks counts from the week of the start date.
  • Worked time comes from start and finish stamps that carry your device's UTC offset, so a shift across a clock change is counted exactly.
  • Route order is a nearest-neighbour suggestion over straight-line distances between the coordinates you enter. It is not a driving route and ignores roads and traffic. No map service is used.
  • Job costing: labor is minutes x each crew member's hourly cost, rounded per entry; materials are expense quantity x unit cost; revenue is invoiced amounts before tax, or the job's line items before it is invoiced. Utilization is job hours / (weekly hours x days / 7).
  • Automatic steps run when you change something and when the app opens. Each one is logged with the reason and can be undone while the records it touched are unchanged; after 60 days only the summary is kept.
  • Nothing is sent for you: reminders open as drafts in your own email or messages app. Payment links point to your own processor; card data never touches this app.
  • Data lives in this browser on this device. Crews work from packet files; there is no live sync. Save backups.
